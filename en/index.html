Subvo

The game's text, on your TV, in your language.

Subvo reads the text in your PS5 game on your iPhone, translates it and shows the translation on your TV, right over the game. No need to put the controller down.

Download on the App Store Source code

Free and ad-free. iPhone, PlayStation 5, and a Samsung or LG TV.

What it translates

Not just subtitles

Besides dialogue, in supported games it also translates in-game phone messages, news, quest cards and the notifications that pop up in the corner.

Dialogue

The speaker's name keeps its in-game color, and the translation covers the game's own subtitle.

Phone messages

Chats opened on the in-game phone are translated right where the message bubbles are.

Cards and notifications

Quest and map cards, messages that slide in from the side and hints in the corner are translated too.

How it works

Console to TV in three steps

1

Your iPhone connects to the console

To your own PS5 over Remote Play. You sign in to your Sony account on Sony's own page; Subvo never sees your password.

2

It reads and translates the text

Reading happens on the phone itself. Translation uses Apple's on-device translation, or your own DeepL or Gemini key if you prefer.

3

It shows it on the TV

The translation appears over the game in the Subvo TV app on the same Wi-Fi. Your phone helps you install the TV app.

In the game

MAYA: The blue car has been following us for three blocks.
RICO: We'll lose them after the bridge.

On the TV (into Spanish)

MAYA: El coche azul nos sigue desde hace tres calles.
RICO: Los perderemos después del puente.

Ready-made translations

Translation without the wait

For some games, a ready-made translation pack (into Turkish) downloads automatically. Lines that are in the pack appear instantly, with no internet needed. The Grand Theft Auto VI pack has more than 738,000 lines and also comes in Arabic.

Grand Theft Auto VI · Turkish, Arabic Red Dead Redemption 2 Grand Theft Auto V Elden Ring + Shadow of the Erdtree Marvel's Spider-Man 2 Death Stranding 2 Death Stranding Director's Cut Assassin's Creed Shadows Mafia: The Old Country Call of Duty: Modern Warfare (2019)

In other games and other languages, the translation engine you choose does the work. There are 12 target languages.

Requirements

Before you start

  • Your own PlayStation 5 and PlayStation account.
  • A Samsung (Tizen) or LG (webOS) smart TV.
  • Phone, console and TV on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • iOS 26 for on-device translation. On older iOS versions you need your own DeepL or Gemini key.

No PS5? Try a sample scene with "See how it works" in the app.

Privacy

Your data stays with you

  • Subvo has no servers; no data goes to the developer.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your PlayStation sign-in and pairing key stay only in the phone's Keychain.
  • Translation is free and fully open. If you'd like to support the app, all you unlock in return is a badge, themes and icons.

Privacy policy

The app

You choose the look

Text size, color, background and layout can be changed in Settings. The app is available in seven languages.

Terms of use (Apple standard EULA)

Frequently asked
  • What do I need? An iPhone (iOS 16 or later), a PS5, and a smart TV on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • How is the translation done? Some games have a ready-made translation pack. Otherwise Apple's on-device translation (iOS 26 or later) or your own DeepL or Gemini key is used.
  • Where does my data go? No data is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I manage a subscription? On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Support purchases are voluntary; every feature of the app is free.
