Subvo

Reads the subtitles of PS5 games on your iPhone, translates them, and sends the translation to a TV on the same Wi-Fi. Free, ad-free and open source.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions: subvo2026@gmail.com

Attaching a recording from Settings › Advanced › "Report a problem" makes it easier to fix. Please also mention your phone model, iOS version and TV brand.

Frequently asked
  • What do I need? An iPhone (iOS 16 or later), a PS5, and a smart TV on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • How is the translation done? Some games have a ready-made translation pack. Otherwise Apple's on-device translation (iOS 26 or later) or your own DeepL or Gemini key is used.
  • Where does my data go? No data is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I manage a subscription? On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Support purchases are voluntary; every feature of the app is free.
